Leave Policy: Every employee gets 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance to your manager.

Working Hours: Official working hours are 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees may work from home on Fridays with manager approval.

Sick Leave: Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 days.

Dress Code: Business casual is expected Monday to Thursday. Casual clothes are allowed on Fridays.

Expense Claims: Work expenses must be submitted within 14 days with receipts attached. Claims are paid at the end of the month.
